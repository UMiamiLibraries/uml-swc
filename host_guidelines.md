# 3. Workshop locations  

### Rosenstiel Library  
When deciding on having the workshop at the Rosenstiel Library, First contact Angela Clark at aclark@earth.miami.edu (Director, Rosenstiel School of Marine, Atmospheric, & Earth Science Library) to check on avalability and reserving the space. There are some limitations such as size constraints, few number of outlets, and lack of accoustics. Number of participants will need to be capped at 25, there will need to be extension chords, and finally ask the IT department at Rosenstiel School of Marine, Atmospheric, and Earth Science for a microphone for instructors.

### CTSI Training Center (Miller School)
Contact Enjoli Johnson at Johnson, Enjoli e.johnson5@miami.edu to coordinate the dates. Leave plemnty of lead time as this space is popular and reserving several days in a row can be challenging if left to the last minute. Make sure to copy Jacqueline Green at jxg350@med.miami.edu (IDSC facilities coordinator) so the proper cleaning arrangements can be made.
